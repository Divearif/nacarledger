The Nácar Workflow

When we open your books, we treat them exactly like we'd treat our own.

That's not a figure of speech — it's the actual standard every account gets held to, and it's why the price isn't a function of how many transactions ran through your account last month.

We look at every transaction, not just the exceptions

Categorization rules help, but they don't replace a human check — the kind of attention we'd want if it were our own account on the line.

We reconcile before we call anything done

Every account ties out to the statement before the books get marked closed — no float, no "close enough for now."

We ask before we file it away

An unusual charge, a missing invoice, a number that doesn't add up — we bring it to you instead of quietly categorizing it and moving on.

You get a conversation, not just a report

Every close ends with a real explanation of what happened this month, in plain language — not a PDF you're left to interpret alone.
