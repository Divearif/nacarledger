REAL ESTATE ACCOUNTING & ADVISORY

Books built for how real estate investors actually operate.

Multi-entity portfolios, transfers between property LLCs, and the contractor relationships that keep a flip or a rental moving — bookkeeping and advisory from someone who's underwritten the deals, not just recorded them after the fact.

What's included

The work that starts once a portfolio is more than one property, one LLC, or one deal.

Multi-entity portfolio accounting

Books kept clean and separate across every property LLC you own, with intercompany balances that actually reconcile at close.

Intercompany transfer & distribution cleanup

Shareholder loans, "my business pays my mortgage" situations, and cross-entity transfers, structured so they hold up to a lender or the IRS.

Contractor & property manager classification

Getting your subs, PMs, and on-site help correctly classified before a 1099 becomes a W-2 problem.

Fix & flip vs. buy & hold bookkeeping

Different accounting realities for a flip that closes in six months versus a rental held for ten years — tracked the way each actually needs.

Cash flow forecasting across a portfolio

A forward view across every property and entity, not just one P&L at a time.

QuickBooks infrastructure for multi-property investors

A chart of accounts and class-tracking structure built to scale as you add properties, not rebuilt every time you do.

Who this is for

This is the right fit if most of the following are true

  • You own more than one property, and more than one LLC holds them
  • Money has moved between your properties, your business, and your personal accounts, and you're not fully sure it was recorded correctly
  • You use contractors, subs, or a property manager and want that relationship reviewed before it's questioned
  • You're closing deals fast enough that basic monthly bookkeeping can't keep up
